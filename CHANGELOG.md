# Changelog

## v0.3

- Add Balanced, Performance, and Battery Saver energy-mode choices for the active power source.
- Request macOS administrator authorization before changing protected power settings.
- Detect supported modes and disable Performance when the Mac or active power source does not support it.
- Show the active energy mode and refresh it along with battery status.

## v0.2

- Replace the carafe with a cat-shaped milk bowl whose milk level tracks battery charge.
- Add a slow, subtle ripple animation to the milk surface.
- Show battery percentage, charging state, and macOS's estimated time remaining or time until full.
- Keep the icon high-contrast in dark and light menu-bar appearances.
- Package as an Apple silicon menu-bar accessory app for macOS 13 and newer.
